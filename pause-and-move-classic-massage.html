Classic Massage in Basel — Time to Truly Switch Off

Classic massage uses long, flowing strokes and gentle kneading to ease tension, relieve pain and leave both your body and mind feeling genuinely restored.

What is Classic Massage?

Simple, effective, deeply relaxing

Classic massage is performed directly on the skin using oil, with smooth rhythmic strokes that warm the muscles and guide you into deep relaxation. It's wonderfully straightforward — and works every time.

Muscle relaxation

Flowing strokes release built-up tension, leaving muscles loose, warm and comfortable.

Pain relief

Gentle kneading eases aches in the back, neck, shoulders and beyond.

A reset for body and mind

The steady rhythm calms the mind as much as the body — many people feel lighter for days.

Better circulation

Stimulates blood flow, helping your body feel warmer, lighter and more energised.

A session typically covers the whole body — back, shoulders, legs, arms — with the pressure and focus adjusted entirely to you. Prefer something firm? Lighter touch? Need extra attention on your shoulders or lower back? Just say so.

Classic massage works beautifully as a standalone treat or as a gentle introduction if you're new to massage therapy. It also pairs wonderfully with more targeted work like deep tissue or Shiatsu.

Insurance

Classic massage sessions are recognized under EMR method 102 (Klassische Massage), ZSR no. C657965. Most Swiss supplementary insurance plans (Zusatzversicherung) reimburse part of the cost — basic insurance (Grundversicherung) doesn't cover complementary therapy at all.

How much comes back, and up to what annual limit, depends on your individual policy. A two-minute call to your insurer with the numbers above will tell you exactly. After the session you'll get a receipt with everything they need.

This applies to classic massage specifically. Shiatsu, Tuina, Connected Movement and Qigong aren't covered by my current registration — if reimbursement matters to you, let me know when you book and we'll work with massage.
